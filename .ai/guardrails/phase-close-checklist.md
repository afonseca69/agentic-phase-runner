# Phase Close Checklist

Before a phase is marked as complete, verify:

- The phase scope was respected.
- Required files were created or updated.
- Acceptance criteria were addressed.
- Relevant tests or checks were executed, or the reason for skipping them was documented.
- The reviewer result is recorded when a reviewer is configured.
- Any gate was either resolved or explicitly left as pending.
- A compact phase summary was written.
- Durable memory, when enabled, contains only the final summary and decisions.
- Temporary logs and raw outputs were not promoted to durable memory.
